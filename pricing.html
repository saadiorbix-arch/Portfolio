Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing, free trial, and subscription terms.

What is the minimum contract duration?
The shortest contract we offer is 2 months. Meaningful SaaS UX work—such as redesigning core flows, structuring a scalable design system, and testing with real users—takes sustained momentum. After the initial 2 months, our contracts convert to flexible month-to-month terms that you can pause or cancel at any time.
How does the 3-day risk-free trial work?
You don't need to provide a credit card or make an upfront payment. We match you with a senior SaaS designer who immediately starts working on an actual flow from your product (typically 1 to 2 high-fidelity screens or an onboarding audit). If you're happy with the speed and quality, we continue directly into your subscription. If not, you walk away with zero obligations.
Can I interview or choose my designer?
Yes. During our intro call, we assess your product's domain, tech stack, and complexity. We match you with a designer who has proven experience in your exact niche (e.g., Fintech, Healthcare, DevTools, or AI). You review their portfolio and speak directly with them before kicking off the 3-day trial.
What happens if my designer gets sick or goes on vacation?
Because all our designers follow unified Eleken design system standards and work under UI/UX Team Lead supervision, we provide seamless coverage. Another vetted senior designer can step in immediately with zero context loss, ensuring your product sprint never stalls.
Will my designer be limited to their timezone?
Not at all. We guarantee a substantial daily working overlap with your core team—typically 4 to 5 hours daily—whether you are based in San Francisco, New York, London, or Singapore. This ensures real-time Slack messaging, video syncs, and same-day design iterations.
What services are included in the subscription?
Our subscription covers the full spectrum of SaaS product design: web & mobile app UI/UX, product redesigns, MVP creation from scratch, UX audits, multi-brand design systems, interactive Figma prototypes, and design engineering alignment (Storybook / ready-to-integrate frontend components).

Test our SaaS design subscription on your own product

Give us a part of your product that's underperforming. We'll dig into the users, evidence, and business goals, identify what we'd change and why, and start working on the solution. See on your own product what an experienced product designer adds on top of AI before committing to a subscription.
