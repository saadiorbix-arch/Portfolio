01

Working Interface

Our designers start with the flows and logic, then use AI to turn them into a working interface fast. Every interaction, state, and visual detail is still shaped by a designer. You can review and comment on the working design just like in Figma.

02

Existing Components

AI builds with the components your product already has, like your tables, buttons, forms, and patterns, instead of creating duplicates. We connect it to your Storybook and review everything it produces. No Storybook yet? We set it up and document your components, because clear descriptions are what help AI use them correctly.

03

Ready for Integration

Once you approve the design, we open a pull request in your repository. Your developers review the code like any other PR, then integrate it with the real product and connect the backend.
